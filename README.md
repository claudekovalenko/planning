# 2027

A plan for the year that moves toward where I sense God leading, held with open
hands (Prov 16:9, 19:21).

## Three screens

- **The year:** the direction for 2027 in one or two sentences, a verse for the
  year, and the vision it serves (read live from the Vision app). Below that, each
  area of life is written as a **from → to**: where I am at the end of 2026, where I
  sense God leading by Dec 31, 2027, and why I believe God is in it. A strip under
  each area fills in month by month from the check-ins.
- **Quarters:** one sentence on what each quarter is for, and a few milestones per
  area. Check them off, rewrite them in place, or move them to another quarter.
- **Check-in:** once a month, mark each area as Toward, Steady or Drifting, and
  answer three questions: what is God saying, where am I drifting, and what one
  step keeps me on the path? The grid at the top shows the whole year at a glance.

It opens with a starting draft written from my own Vision, growth edges and
Centered decisions (planted, marriage). Tap any text to rewrite it, or choose
"Start blank instead".

## Running it

Open `index.html`. There is no build step. GitHub Pages deploys it from this
branch via `.github/workflows/pages.yml` to
<https://claudekovalenko.github.io/planning/>. Over HTTPS it can be installed as an
app and works offline.

Data stays in the browser under `planning.year.v1`. The vision is read from
`vision-app-v2`, which works because the apps share the same origin. Use
"Save a backup" and "Load a backup" to move the plan between devices.
