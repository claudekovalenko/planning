# Five-Year Plan

Two parts, switched at the top: **Plan** (5 years, 50 years, 2027) and
**Timeline**, which has its own address (`#timeline`). It opens on the
five-year plan:

- **5 years:** the direction for the five years, then a few areas of life — Church home
  first, then Teaching & digital ministry, then Disciple-making growing out of it, then Walk with God, Marriage & family,
  Health, Provision. For each area: where I want to be by 2031, and a milestone
  for the end of each year from 2027 to 2030. Tap any cell to write in it.
  Each year shows the age I will be (born 1995).
- **Timeline:** the big things on one straight line. At the top, the two
  biggest things (finding my church and whom I marry), then the next big
  milestones with how far away they are and my age. The line zooms to 10, 20,
  50 or 60 years and shows the big ones by default, or every milestone. Below it,
  what is happening now. Milestones are edited behind "Edit milestones": the
  star marks a big one, and two stars one of the biggest. The marriage card
  carries a "Ready for marriage" checklist: strength gain, the car, sufficient
  income, free from lust, clear on my call, clear on my church home.
- **50 years:** where it is all heading, then each area by 2031, 2036, 2046,
  2066 and 2076, with ages.
- **2027:** the dated goals for this year, grouped by area under that area's
  2027 milestone. Each goal is a bar across Jan–Dec from its start date to its
  deadline, with days left, overdue goals shown in red, and a line marking today.
  Tap a goal to change its dates, its area or its name.

It opens with a starting draft drawn from the Vision app. Rewrite it, or choose
"Start blank".

Live at <https://claudekovalenko.github.io/planning/>, deployed by GitHub Pages
from this branch. No build step: just open `index.html`. Data saves in the
browser under `planning.five.v1`.
